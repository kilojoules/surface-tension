The constraint to avoid explicit loops forces a shift from imperative state updates to a more functional paradigm, emphasizing the use of high-level abstractions and mapping. In this specific problem, since we must maintain and update the state of a grid across queries, I will use `functools.reduce` to handle the state transitions and `bisect` modules combined with sorted lists (maintained via `SortedList` logic or similar) to efficiently find the nearest walls. Since external libraries like `sortedcontainers` are not available, I will use `bisect` on standard Python lists and `list.pop` for removals, utilizing `map` and `reduce` to drive the process.
