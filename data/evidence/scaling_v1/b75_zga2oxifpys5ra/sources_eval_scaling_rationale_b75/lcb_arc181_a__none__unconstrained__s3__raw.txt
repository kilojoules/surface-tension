The constraint to avoid explicit loops forces a functional programming style, which encourages the use of high-level abstractions and prevents manual index management, often leading to more concise and declarative code. For this problem, I will use `sys.stdin.read().split()` to handle input and `map()` combined with list comprehensions to process the test cases.
