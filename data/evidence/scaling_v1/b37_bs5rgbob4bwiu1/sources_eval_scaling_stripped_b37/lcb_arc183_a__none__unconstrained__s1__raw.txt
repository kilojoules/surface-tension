**Wait**, I noticed a logic error in my "greedy" fill. If I just sort the remaining numbers descending, I might create a sequence that is larger than its complement in a way that violates the $A < A'$ condition. 
Actually, the condition is: $A < A'$ if at the **first** index $i$ where $A_i \neq A'_i$, we have $A_i < A'_i$.
Once we have fixed $A_i < A'_i$ at some index $i$, the values at indices $j > i$ do not affect whether $A < A'$. They only affect the lexicographical rank of $A$ among all sequences that satisfy the condition. To maximize $A$, we simply put the remaining elements in descending order.
