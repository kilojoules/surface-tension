For each pair, the `sum` iterates $N/d$ times. This could be slow in Python. However, the total number of checks across all $d$ for a fixed $i$ is $N$. The complexity is roughly $O(N^2 \log N)$ or $O(N^2)$ depending on the distribution of heights. Given the 2s time limit, this should pass.
