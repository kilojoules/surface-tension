The constraint to avoid explicit loops forces a shift toward functional programming paradigms, which can lead to more concise and declarative code by focusing on *what* to compute rather than *how* to iterate. For this problem, I will use slicing and comparison logic combined with `any()` and list comprehensions to check all possible single-edit scenarios (substitution, insertion, deletion) without using `for` or `while` loops.
