The constraint to avoid explicit loops forces a shift from imperative state management to a more functional approach. While not necessarily "cleaner" for state-heavy problems, it encourages the use of higher-order functions and immutable-style transformations. To solve this, I will use `functools.reduce` to maintain the state (the current total height offset and a sorted list of plant "birth" offsets) across the sequence of queries. I will use `bisect_left` to efficiently find and remove plants that have reached the required height.
