Final check on constraints: $N=3000$. The complexity is $O(H \cdot (\text{avg\_group\_size}^2 \cdot N))$. In the worst case where all buildings have the same height, it's $O(N^3)$. $3000^3$ is too slow. 

Actually, the number of elements $k$ we need to check is limited by $N/d$. 
The total number of pairs $(i, j)$ across all groups is at most $N(N-1)/2$. 
For each pair, we check $k$ from $1$ to $N/d$.
The total complexity is $\sum_{h} \sum_{i < j} \frac{N}{idx_j - idx_i}$.
This is generally acceptable for $N=3000$ if the distribution of heights is varied, but $O(N^3)$ is the theoretical worst case. However, given the constraints and the "no loop" rule, comprehensions are the only way.
